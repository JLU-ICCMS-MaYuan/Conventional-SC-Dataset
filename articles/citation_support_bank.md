1. Keep 2026 and arXiv items clearly dated as current local/preprint context.
2. Do not use benchmark citations to imply that Conventional-SC-Dataset already has benchmark splits.
3. Do not use FAIR citations to claim full FAIR compliance unless the manuscript adds persistent release metadata and archive policy.
