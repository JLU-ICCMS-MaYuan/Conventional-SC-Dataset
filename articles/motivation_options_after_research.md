# Motivation Options After Research

## Option A: Auditable Curation Platform

Conventional-SC-Dataset addresses the operational gap between superconductivity literature and reusable data by providing an auditable, element-centric platform that links papers, multiple physical-parameter records, screenshot evidence, contributor identity, administrator review, and chart-display control.

**Recommendation:** Choose this as the controlling motivation. It is specific, strongly supported by local evidence, and does not overclaim discovery or prediction.

## Option B: Bridge Between Databases And Literature Workflow

Conventional-SC-Dataset bridges materials/structure databases and superconductivity-specific datasets by converting paper-level superconductivity evidence into reviewed element-system records suitable for browsing, visualization, and downstream dataset construction.

**Use if:** The paper should foreground the literature comparison and introduction more than implementation details.

## Option C: Data Infrastructure For Future Superconductivity AI

Conventional-SC-Dataset provides the curation infrastructure needed before reliable superconductivity machine-learning datasets can be assembled, by separating physical-parameter records from paper metadata and preserving reviewable provenance.

**Use with caution:** This is attractive, but it risks making reviewers expect benchmark splits, model evaluation, and data-release policy. Only use if future AI framing is important.

## Option D: Web Software Paper

Conventional-SC-Dataset is a web-based scientific software platform for browsing, submitting, reviewing, and visualizing superconductivity literature records organized by element combinations.

**Use if:** The target venue is a software/system venue rather than a database/resource venue.

## Recommended Confirmed Motivation Draft

Conventional-SC-Dataset provides an auditable, element-centric superconductivity literature and physical-property curation platform that links DOI-based paper records, multiple physical-parameter entries, screenshot evidence, user roles, administrator review, and controlled visualization, filling an operational gap left between general materials databases, structure databases, and superconductivity-specific datasets.
