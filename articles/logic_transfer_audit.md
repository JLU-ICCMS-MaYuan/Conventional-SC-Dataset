# Logic Transfer Audit

## Source Logic

The source materials frame Conventional-SC-Dataset as a database-oriented website that connects element systems, superconductivity literature, physical parameters, evidence screenshots, user roles, administrator review, visualization control, and maintenance scripts.

Key source anchors:

- `docs/business-overview.md`: system positioning and module boundaries.
- `docs/article-draft.md`: Chinese article plan, initial manuscript logic, claim-evidence table, workflow and ER diagrams, code/data statistics.
- `docs/current-state-and-gaps.md`: implemented capabilities, partial capabilities, and unimplemented community/database-release features.
- `articles/research_dossier.md`: target scene and bounded resource/database/software-system framing.
- `articles/confirmed_motivation.md`: user-confirmed Option A.

## Unsupported Claim Check

No final manuscript claim intentionally asserts:

- a new superconducting mechanism,
- a validated Tc-prediction model,
- implemented community features,
- a frozen public benchmark,
- a finalized public data release,
- full FAIR compliance,
- or production deployment at community scale.

Remaining placeholders are explicit rather than fabricated: repository URL, affiliation, author contribution, competing interests confirmation, grants, and release DOI.

## Revision Trace

The final manuscript remains faithful to the confirmed motivation while adding reviewer-driven support:

- It keeps the platform contribution as auditable curation infrastructure.
- It narrows scale-sensitive claims to repository/database snapshots.
- It adds comparison and reproducibility scaffolding instead of inflating results.
- It records submission blockers rather than inventing missing metadata.

## Result

PASS for logic transfer from local materials to final manuscript draft.
